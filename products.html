For the enterprise buyer

Why an organisation with auditors says yes.

Nothing irreversible without approval

Every send, post and payment stops at the permission ring until a named human approves it. The ordering of that check is covered by automated tests, including the case where trusted mode must still refuse a forbidden action. Control is enforced, not promised.

Your data stays yours

Enterprise OS deploys on your infrastructure: your servers, your region, your residency rules. The memory graph, the ledgers and the backups live inside your estate. Leaving takes your data with you, because it never left.

Staff that passed an exam

Every department is briefed to its craft, given written boundaries, and examined before it touches your business. Failing hires are corrected and retake. The transcripts are kept with your deployment, which is more than most human onboarding leaves behind.

Watchable, in real time

The live stream names what the system is touching: the file, the record, the recipient. A wrong move is catchable while it happens, not discovered in an audit three months later. Credentials are never displayed, by design.

It integrates, it does not replace

Your ERP stays. Dynamics, SAP Business One, Odoo, Xero, your CRM and your storefronts connect through official interfaces on your own licences. We operate your systems; we do not ask you to abandon them.

Proven on ourselves first

TSB Labs runs on Madar OS daily: its CRM, its outreach, its follow ups, its own board reviews. We are deployment zero, and the measurements, including the unproven parts, are published in our case studies.

What we will not claim: certifications we do not hold, uptime histories we have not accumulated, and reference customers that do not exist yet. Enterprise OS is early, the engineering discipline behind it is real, and the fastest way to judge it is to watch it run our company live on a call.
